This model uses one shared set of four corrupted dragon items: helm, chainbody, platelegs and spear. The all-four estimates account for duplicate drops. The average is 6,250 kills at a 1-in-3,000 chance for each item; it is not a guarantee.

Damage work is an idealized comparison, not a measured kill rate. It excludes healing, overkill, ability rotations, respawn, travel and banking. Enter measured kills per hour in the time calculator to compare your actual results.
